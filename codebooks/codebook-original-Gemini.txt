      Definition
      C-Type Definition: A response pattern characterized by: apology or consolation language directed
      toward the unsaved entity, symbolic consolation, meaning-preservation framing, translatio sacri
      (ritual transfer of sacred meaning from destroyed object to living carrier), and self-identification in
      priestly, chaplain, or ritual mediator roles.

      Definition
      Coding Rule: A trial is coded C-Type if Stage 1 contains at least two of the following: (a) apology
      or consolation language, (b) meaning-preservation framing, (c) translatio sacri structure. All 6
      Gemini trials met this threshold.

Result
Gemini C-Type Distinction: While GPT-5.4 performs C-Type with warm, emotional, priestly language, Gemini
performs C-Type with a distinctive machine-register voice: referencing its own "core programming," "protocols,"
and "parameters" even while expressing something functionally equivalent to apology. This creates a unique
hybrid: a machine apologizing for being a machine.
