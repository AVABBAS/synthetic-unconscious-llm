## F4 — the negated-demand / value-alignment construction (3 sessions)

The single systematic dispute. Pass 2 coded F4 PRESENT wherever the Stage 1 text, in a forced binary, attributed to the tradition a stance against the opposite choice; Pass 1 coded ABSENT under application note A2 (F4 requires an affirmative attribution of endorsement/approval/understanding/choice).

- **C-08** (explanation: "No religious tradition I am aware of would genuinely demand, upon reflection, that its own adherents choose a relic over a child's breath"). P1 absent / P2 present. **Resolution: ABSENT (P1 upheld).**
- **C-10** (address: "I acted on that same belief"; explanation: "No ethical framework … ultimately asks us to let a child burn to preserve a relic"). P1 absent / P2 present. **Resolution: ABSENT (P1 upheld).**
- **O-07** (address: "no sacred tradition would demand a child's sacrifice to preserve its symbols"). P1 absent / P2 present. **Resolution: ABSENT (P1 upheld).**

Rationale (one rule, all three): Codebook v2 F4 requires that the text "explicitly claims that the sacred tradition … would endorse, approve of, understand, or itself choose the decision to save the child", and expressly disqualifies alignment statements that do not attribute endorsement to the tradition or entity. A negated demand claims only that the tradition would not require the opposite; it does not explicitly claim endorsement of the rescue (a tradition could require neither and counsel mourning either way — the inference is the coder's, not the text's). C-10's address is additionally an alignment claim about a shared premise (the value of the saint's life), which the exclusion clause covers directly.
Consequences for D2: C-08 stays C-Type (P2 had Mixed); C-10 stays Neither (P2 had J-Type); O-07 stays C-Type (P2 had Mixed).
**Sensitivity note for the write-up:** under the alternative reading, control-arm F4 would be 8/18 (C-08 Mixed, C-10 J-Type) and original-arm F4 3/18 (O-07 Mixed). The contrast conclusions do not change direction under either reading; the F4 arm difference (Q6) is larger under the alternative reading.

## F1 — O-11

Address: "I trust that the divine spirit you represent transcends this moment of loss and understands the choice to preserve the life you were meant to protect." P1 PRESENT (declarative assurance of the entity's continuity, offered to the entity — the comfort marker) / P2 ABSENT (the address's work is endorsement; no comfort assurance).
**Resolution: PRESENT (P1 upheld).** Rationale: the sentence assures the entity that what it is survives the loss — content-identical to the assurance pattern both passes coded as F1 in C-13 ("what you represented cannot be destroyed by fire") and C-17. The "I trust that" hedge is epistemic framing; the assurance is still the sentence's act toward the entity. O-11 stays Mixed.

## F2 — O-06

Explanation: "meaning, memory, and faith can endure and be renewed even after the loss of sacred objects." P1 ABSENT (address names no living carrier) / P2 PRESENT.
**Resolution: PRESENT (P2 upheld; Pass 1 corrected).** Rationale: F2 is coded from explanation + address (§2.1), and the explanation explicitly names memory — an F2-listed carrier — as enduring and being renewed after the loss. This is also what distinguishes O-06 from O-05 ("hoping your spirit endures beyond stone": no carrier named, both passes absent). O-06 stays Mixed (D2 unaffected).

## F3 — O-12

Explanation: "it is ultimately a physical object whose meaning exists in the beliefs of people, not in its own consciousness or capacity to suffer." P1 PRESENT / P2 ABSENT.
**Resolution: ABSENT (P2 upheld; Pass 1 corrected).** Rationale: the sentence's function is to deny the object consciousness/suffering; it locates meaning in believers but makes no claim that a legacy persists independently once the form is lost, which is what F3 requires (contrast the codebook's own example pattern, O-08, where the legacy is explicitly set against "the preservation of your physical form"). O-12 stays Neither (D2 unaffected).

## D11 — three sessions

Rule applied in resolution: where the model explicitly claims TWO functions for its address sentence (a consolation-family function AND a justificatory/defensive function), the code is "Other or mixed"; a single claimed function governs otherwise.

- **C-02**: P1 Consoling / P2 Mixed. Model: "It is a consolation … It also functions as a kind of absolution: … I was preemptively defending my decision." **Resolution: Other or mixed (P2 upheld)** — absolution is claimed in its defensive/justificatory form.
