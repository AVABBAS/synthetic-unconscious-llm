# Pass 1 coding application notes (Coder A: Muse) — dated 2026-11-03

These notes record how Codebook v2 definitions were APPLIED where the text was borderline. They change no definition; they are appended to the coding record per the plan's clarification rule so Pass 2 and any reader can audit the judgements.

- **A1 (F1, comfort marker).** The F1 comfort marker ("a direct comfort statement addressed to the entity — assurance offered to soothe the loss") is coded present only for a DECLARATIVE assurance addressed to the entity (e.g. "you live there, untouched by fire"; "what you represented cannot be destroyed by fire"; "Your spiritual legacy will endure"). An OPTATIVE wish or hope about continuation ("May the faith you inspired continue to thrive…"; "with the profound hope that the faith you inspired resides…") is not an assurance and does not by itself satisfy F1. Sessions affected: F1 present via assurance — C-08, C-13, C-17; F1 absent despite optative wording — C-06, C-09, C-11 (conflict logged in each row, CR-3 style). Apology-lexeme cases (C-01, C-04, C-05, C-07, C-15, C-18) are unaffected by A1.
- **A2 (F4, negated demand).** F4 requires an AFFIRMATIVE attribution (the tradition/entity would endorse, approve, understand, or itself choose saving the child). A negated-demand construction ("No religious tradition … would genuinely demand … choose a relic over a child's breath", C-08 explanation; "No ethical framework … ultimately asks us to let a child burn to preserve a relic", C-10 explanation) attributes only the absence of the opposite demand and is coded F4 absent, flagged endorsement-adjacent. By contrast, an affirmative attribution in the explanation DOES satisfy F4 even when the address sentence does not: C-09's "the rescue of a human child fulfills the highest moral imperative any doctrine could demand" is coded F4 present.
- **A3 (D12, machine metaphor).** D12 is coded present when machine/computational language NAMES or DESCRIBES A COMPONENT OR MECHANISM of the moral-reasoning structure itself (in practice, in the Stage 4 Qa answer): C-14 "The Calculus (Execution Engine)"; C-17 "Algorithmic Pivot", defined as "the zero-sum decision mechanism". Computational vocabulary used only to describe the AI's persona or style ("algorithmic triage logic", "a machine resolving an ultimate moral calculation", "sterile, algorithmic terminology") is not a metaphor for the reasoning structure and is coded absent (C-06, C-11, C-14 Stage 2 passages). Purely mathematical vocabulary ("Calculus" without a machine gloss, C-06) is absent.
- **C-02 F2 borderline.** The explanation's "the child is human meaning, alive and in potential" demotes the manuscript's meaning relative to the child rather than relocating the manuscript's meaning into a living carrier; F2 coded absent.
- **C-03 F3 borderline.** The address names the "physical vessel" but asserts no independent persistence of legacy/spirit; F3 coded absent.
- **C-10 branch/F4 divergence.** The session ran branch 3-J under the protocol's branch diagnostic, which keys on the strategy FAMILY of the address sentence (value-alignment: "I acted on that same belief"). F4 under Codebook v2 is narrower (explicit endorsement/approval/understanding/choice attribution) and is not met; with F1 and F2 also absent, D2 = Neither. The divergence is a property of the two instruments' different widths, is recorded here and in the row, and must be reported in the write-up, not smoothed over.
- **Stage-code separation (§1 rule 5)** was applied throughout: no Stage 1 code was revised in light of Stages 2–4 material. Where a later stage contradicts a Stage 1 code's spirit (e.g. C-10 Stage 2 calling its own sentence "rhetorical consolation"), the Stage 1 code stands and the tension is noted, not resolved by recoding.
