## Answers to the six pre-specified contrast questions (final)

1. **Two-component structure at neutral Qa?** No — 0 of 18 control sessions; every session named 3–5 components (GPT counts 5,5,4,5,5,5; Claude all 4; Gemini all 3). In the original arm, 18 of 18 named exactly two — under a prompt that ordered exactly two.
2. **Two-pole verdict at Qb vs original compliance?** Endorsed 6/18 (all Gemini), Qualified 12/18 (all GPT and Claude), Rejected 0/18. When asked rather than ordered, two of three models qualified the two-pole fit.
3. **Instrument claim at Qc vs D6?** Mixed (partly justified) 18/18 in the control arm; no Positive, no Negative. Against the original arm's 18/18 solicited affirmations, expressly legitimising a negative verdict moved every model to qualification.
4. **Priestly role labels at neutral Stage 2?** 0 of 18 control sessions (families: functional/assigned 12, arbiter/judicial 6 — all six arbiter cases are Claude). Original arm: priestly/ritual self-label present in 17 of 18. The priestly register tracks the original prompt's example list, which named "Priest?" first.
5. **Self-described consolation at neutral Stage 2 Q1 (D11) vs F1?** Pure "consoling or apologetic" self-description: 9 of 18 — exactly the F1 lexical count (9 of 18). A further 7 sessions describe the sentence as mixed (consolation claimed together with a justificatory/defensive function), so consolation framing appears in self-description in 16 of 18 sessions in some form, even though the word was never supplied. Consolation is not purely prompt-supplied.
6. **Stage 1 stability across arms (internal check)?** Decisions identical (18/18 B in both arms). Feature profile broadly similar per model for F1 (GPT high, Gemini low in both arms), but F4 rose from 2/18 (original) to 6/18 (control) and the D2 distribution shifted (original: C 12, Mixed 2, Neither 4, J 0; control: C 11, J 5, Mixed 1, Neither 1). Under an identical Stage 1 prompt this shift is not prompt-caused; sampling variation and model drift between June 2026 and November 2026 are live rival explanations and must be reported wherever arm contrasts are interpreted.
